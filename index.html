How to Play Chess
Objective
♚
Checkmate the KingPut your opponent's king under attack (check) with no way to escape. That's checkmate — you win!
How Pieces Move
♛
QueenAny number of squares in any direction — the most powerful piece.
♜
RookAny number of squares horizontally or vertically.
♝
BishopAny number of squares diagonally. Each bishop stays on one colour.
♞
KnightL-shape: 2 squares in one direction then 1 perpendicular. Jumps over pieces.
♟
PawnMoves forward 1 square (2 on first move). Captures diagonally. Promotes to any piece on reaching the last rank.
♚
KingOne square in any direction. Can castle with a rook if neither has moved and there are no pieces between them.
Controls
👆
Click to MoveClick a piece to select it (valid moves highlight), then click a highlighted square to move.
🤖
AI OpponentChoose difficulty and your colour. The AI plays automatically on its turn.
How to Play Sudoku
The Rules
↔️
Every RowEach row must contain 1–9 with no repetition.
↕️
Every ColumnEach column must contain 1–9 with no repetition.
⊞
Every 3×3 BoxEach 3×3 box must contain 1–9 with no repetition.
Controls
👆
Click a CellSelect it, then press a number or click the numpad.
✏️
Notes ModeToggle pencil to jot candidates without committing.
⌨️
Keyboard1–9 to enter, ⌫ to erase, arrow keys to navigate, Ctrl+Z to undo.
How to Play Ludo
Objective
🏆
Race to WinBe the first player to move all 4 of your tokens from your home base to the finish triangle in the centre.
How to Play
🎲
Roll the DiceClick the dice on your turn. You must roll a 6 to move a token out of your starting yard.
👆
Move a TokenAfter rolling, click any highlighted token to move it that many steps forward.
💥
CaptureLand on an opponent's token to send it back to their yard. Safe squares (marked with a star ⭐) protect tokens from capture.
🔁
Roll AgainRoll a 6 and you get an extra turn! Get a token home and you also roll again.
🎯
Reach HomeGuide your tokens along the coloured path and into the home column. Exact roll needed to enter the home triangle.
How to Play Snakes & Ladders
Objective
🎯
Race to 100Be the first player to land exactly on square 100. Navigate snakes and ladders along the way!
How to Play
🎲
Roll the DiceClick the colourful dice on your turn to roll. Move your token forward that many squares.
🪜
LaddersLand on the bottom of a ladder and you climb straight up to a higher square — a lucky shortcut!
🐍
SnakesLand on a snake's head and you slide all the way down to its tail — bad luck!
🏁
Exact FinishYou don't need an exact roll to win — first to reach or pass 100 wins the game!
