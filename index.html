How to Play

Sudoku is a logic-based number puzzle. Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with the digits 1–9 — each digit appearing exactly once. No maths required, just pure logic.

The Rules
↔️
Every Row Each of the 9 horizontal rows must contain the digits 1–9 with no repetition.
↕️
Every Column Each of the 9 vertical columns must contain the digits 1–9 with no repetition.
⊞
Every 3×3 Box Each of the nine 3×3 sub-grids must contain the digits 1–9 with no repetition.
🔒
Given Digits Bright white digits are pre-filled clues — they cannot be changed. Use them as anchors to solve the rest.
How to Enter Numbers
👆
Click a Cell Tap or click any empty cell to select it. It will highlight in purple, and all related cells (same row, column, box) will be subtly shaded.
🔢
Enter the Number Click the number buttons below the board, or press 1–9 on your keyboard. An incorrect entry will flash red.
✏️
Pencil / Notes Mode Toggle the Pencil switch in the left panel to jot small candidate numbers inside a cell without committing to an answer.
Keyboard Shortcuts

⌫ / DelErase the selected cell
↑ ↓ ← →Navigate between cells
